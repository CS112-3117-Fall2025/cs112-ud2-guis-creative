Getting Started

To start tracking your workouts, go to the Home page and log your exercises. You can add new workouts and see your progress at any time.

Tips

  • Log exercises consistently for best results
  • Review your progress regularly
  • Set goals and track improvements

Support

If you have issues with the app, check that all your entries are correctly formatted and saved. It can also help to refresh the page if needed.
